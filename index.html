TL;DR

FailPatch introduces a failure-driven paradigm for adapting frozen VLA policies from deployment failures, achieving consistent gains across simulation and real-world settings with only 0.52% trainable parameters.

Abstract

Vision-Language-Action (VLA) policies are typically adapted using successful demonstrations, which provide direct action supervision but rarely cover failure-prone states. Deployment failures expose these states, yet lack the corrective actions needed for conventional supervised learning.

We propose FailPatch, a failure-driven residual patching framework that decouples action supervision from execution-reliability supervision. Successful demonstrations ground how the policy should act, while deployment trajectories indicate when its behavior becomes unreliable. FailPatch introduces a Null-gated Residual Expert Bank into the action-hidden space of a frozen VLA policy. A unified Preserve-Redirect-Trust objective retains the original policy in reliable states, selects residual experts in failure-associated states, and redirects representations toward success-associated regions under bounded intervention.

With only 0.52% trainable parameters, FailPatch improves success rates by 11.0 percentage points on four long-horizon RoboTwin tasks under clean evaluation, 9.5 points under clean-to-random generalization, and 16.7 points over the baseline across three real-world tasks.

Framework

Experiments

We evaluate FailPatch in both long-horizon simulation and physical dual-arm manipulation. The experiments test clean performance, generalization under unseen visual and physical randomization, real-world transfer, parameter efficiency, and the mechanism behind localized residual intervention.

Simulation Results

Across four long-horizon RoboTwin tasks, FailPatch raises the average clean success rate from 49.5% to 60.5%. It also improves clean-to-random evaluation from 28.0% to 37.5%, showing that failure-derived supervision transfers beyond the exact deployment conditions used for adaptation.

Unlike PPO, which requires fresh online interaction, FailPatch adapts from a fixed offline deployment buffer. It updates only the router and residual experts—17.3M parameters—while keeping the VLA backbone and action projection frozen.

Real-World Manipulation

We further evaluate rigid-object ordering, contact-rich bowl stacking, and deformable dishcloth folding on a physical dual-arm platform. FailPatch achieves the best result on every task and improves average success from 41.1% for the three-task base policy to 57.8%.

Mechanistic Analysis

Action hidden representations provide the most effective intervention interface: a linear probe separates nominal and failure-associated chunks with 0.74 balanced accuracy and 0.83 ROC-AUC, outperforming frozen visual features and predicted actions. This space is both outcome-sensitive and directly connected to action generation.

The residual experts also learn distinct correction directions rather than collapsing to a single behavior. Low off-diagonal cosine similarities appear in both hidden residuals and their projected action residuals, while the Null route lets the model abstain in reliable states.
